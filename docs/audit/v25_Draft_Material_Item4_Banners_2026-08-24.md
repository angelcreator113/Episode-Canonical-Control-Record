## §1 What item 4 asks, and what it is actually testing

v24 Sec 6 item 4: read the newest correction banners in `F-AUTH-1_Fix_Plan_v2.59.md`,
`Prime_Studios_Audit_Handoff_v23.md`, XK-1, and
`FD-66_Model_Migration_Contract_Mismatch_2026-08-18_DRAFT.md` **before their body
sections.**

Under additive-supersede a banner prepends and the body is never edited. **A
reader who starts at the body reads a superseded value as current.** Item 4 is
the operational countermeasure to exactly that.

So the test is not *what does the banner say*. It is: **does the banner change
what the body asserts, and would a body-first reader get a wrong answer?**
