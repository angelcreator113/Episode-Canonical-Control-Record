# §D1. What was proposed, and what is ruled

**§C5 of Amendment 3 records that the only mechanism demonstrated to have
arrested ruling-in-passing is a second party noticing at the moment of
deferral.**

By 2026-08-23 that mechanism had two demonstrations in a single session: it
caught a deferral of three derivations, and it caught the partial read of the
record that produced the superseded limb 1 count.

**It was proposed that this be treated as the register's operating assumption.**

> **RULED: no. §C5's observation is not promoted to an operating assumption.**

---

# §D2. Three reasons

**An operating assumption is something you build on. This one cannot bear
weight.**

## §D2.1 No measured failure rate

**Two demonstrations in one session is a small sample of a mechanism nobody has
characterised.** Neither its rate nor its conditions are known. **Nothing
routed either catch** — the second party asked a question about retrievability
and the gap surfaced incidentally. **An unrouted catch is not a mechanism; it is
an event.**

## §D2.2 Its failures are invisible by construction

**This is the decisive reason.**

> **An uncaught deferral looks exactly like no deferral. An uncaught partial
> read looks exactly like a complete one.**

**The mechanism therefore cannot be observed failing.** Every recorded instance
is a success, because failures leave no trace to record. **A success rate
computed over a population that excludes all failures is not a success rate**,
and treating one as evidence is the §A3 shape applied to a process rather than
an instrument: **absence of observed failure returned as absence of failure.**

## §D2.3 Promotion would assert a control the register does not have

**Naming the second party as an operating assumption converts an observation
about what has happened into a control the register can rely on.** It has no
such control.

**This is the failure mode `Attribution_Gap_2026-08-22_DRAFT.md` §6 names:** *a
reader who sees "forward remedy" will hear "addressed."* A reader who sees a
mechanism named as an operating assumption will hear that the failure is
handled. **It is not handled.**

**Amendment 3 §C5 already declined this**, by recording the finding and
proposing no procedure. **This ruling holds that line against the recurrence.**

# §D3. The boundary — what is NOT ruled

**This ruling is against promotion. It is not against the observation.**

**§C5 stands exactly as written.** The second party remains **the only thing
demonstrated to have arrested this failure**, and that remains worth recording,
because it is true and because no alternative has been demonstrated.

**A future author reading this amendment must not conclude that §C5 was
withdrawn, weakened, or superseded.** What is ruled out is **relying** on it —
building a procedure, a checklist, or a gate that presumes the second party will
be there.

**No procedure is proposed here either**, for the same reason Amendment 3 gave:
**none has been demonstrated to work.**

---

# §D4. Why this ruling is filed rather than carried

**The temptation to promote this finding will recur, and a ruling that exists
only in a transcript cannot resist a recurrence.**

**Flattering findings get upgraded.** *"A second party caught it"* reads as
evidence that the process is sound — attentive, self-correcting, working as
intended. **That reading is why the promotion was proposed, and it is why it
will be proposed again.** The reasons in §D2 are not obvious on re-encounter;
they have to be re-derived by anyone who meets the finding fresh, and the
flattering reading is available immediately while the objection is not.

**Recording the objection is therefore load-bearing in a way the finding is
not.**

**This amendment was itself a ruling-in-passing.** It was given in the course of
reviewing a correction, and the first instinct was to defer it until a second
item accumulated to carry it with. **"When there is something to pair it with"
has no occasion attached** — the same defect as §B3's *"when it's next touched."*
**Sixth occurrence across three sessions.** Filed immediately instead, alone,
which is what §B3 requires.
