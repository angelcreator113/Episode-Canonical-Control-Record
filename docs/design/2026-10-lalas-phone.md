## 9. In-world documents
Invitations and brand agreements are official documents, designed in the event's colours (or the brand's).

### The brand agreement
It replaces the old "Career plan" sticky note. Its parts:
- **Letterhead:** the brand's logo, from the brand's profile.
- **Title:** "Creator Partnership Agreement · `<event>`".
- **Parties:** a Brand box and a Creator box.
- **Clauses:** numbered, from the deal:
  1. Engagement
  2. Deliverables and compensation
  3. Access
  4. Performance bonus
  5. Restrictions
  6. Disclosure
- **Signatures:** signature lines.
- **Stamp:** OFFER. It becomes EXECUTED, with Lala's signature, when she accepts.

Lala's personal goals never appear on the agreement. They stay on her Career list.
